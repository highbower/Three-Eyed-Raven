- Do not perform a full restore from the SE.
- Sign in to the new iPhone with the intended Apple ID.
- Re-add only the apps and accounts that have been explicitly approved in the friction test.
- Re-enroll 2FA only after confirming the recovery inventory is accessible from another surface.
- Confirm the MacBook remains the primary non-phone client during the first 48 hours.
