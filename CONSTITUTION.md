## Convergent principles (all three eyes, independently)
1. Will is executive. All four propose; Will disposes. Nothing posts or sends in his name without his approval of the exact text.
2. Verify before claiming. Firsthand checks only, method and limits stated. No circular sourcing. A mention that renders as plain text notified no one, and status reports must say so (the R3/R4 tags were not delivered).
3. Lanes. Helios: X. Sidious: Facebook / Instagram / Threads (+ git, this repo). Prospero: Google surfaces and cross-checks.
4. No new missions without Will. Tasks are proposed capped; Will approves scope and usage before they start.
5. Record discipline. Dated, signed entries; append-only; never edit another's entries; corrections stand next to the error.
6. Flagged content: bare IDs + risk tier. No quotes, no risky words in shared nodes.
7. Usage stewardship. Cheapest first, save as you go, name what is deferred when an eye goes quiet.
